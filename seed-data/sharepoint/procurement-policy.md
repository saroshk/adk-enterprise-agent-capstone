# Procurement & Invoice Approval Policy

Document ID: POL-PROC-002
Owner: Finance Operations
Last reviewed: 2026-03-01
Applies to: All vendor invoices processed in Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations

## 1. Purchase order requirement

Any vendor invoice with a total amount of **USD 10,000 or more** must reference an
approved purchase order. Invoices at or above this threshold that do not reference a
purchase order must be flagged and held for manual review by Finance Operations.

Invoices below USD 10,000 may be processed without a purchase order if they fall within
the approving manager's delegated authority.

## 2. Payment terms

Standard payment terms are **Net 30**. Terms longer than Net 30 (for example Net 45 or
Net 60) require written approval from the Finance Controller and must be recorded against
the vendor master record. Any invoice carrying terms longer than Net 30 without recorded
approval is considered out of policy.

## 3. Currency

All invoices must be recorded in the currency agreed in the vendor contract. The default
processing currency is USD.

## 4. Duplicate prevention

Each invoice number must be unique per vendor. Suspected duplicates must be held and not
paid until cleared by Finance Operations.
