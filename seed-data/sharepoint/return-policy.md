# Supplier Returns & Credit Policy

Document ID: POL-RET-004
Owner: Procurement
Last reviewed: 2026-02-12

## Return window

Goods received from suppliers may be returned within **30 days** of the recorded goods
receipt date, provided they are unused and in original packaging.

## Approval

Returns valued at USD 5,000 or more require approval from the Procurement Lead before a
return merchandise authorization (RMA) is issued.

## Credit notes

Once a return is received and inspected by the supplier, a credit note must be issued
within 14 business days and applied against the next open invoice for that vendor.

## Restocking fees

A restocking fee of up to 15% may apply to non-defective returns, at the supplier's
discretion as defined in the vendor contract.
