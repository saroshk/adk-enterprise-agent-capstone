# Vendor Onboarding FAQ

Document ID: FAQ-VEN-009
Owner: Procurement
Last reviewed: 2026-01-20

## How do I add a new vendor?

Submit the vendor request form to Finance Operations. A vendor master record is created
in Dynamics 365 F&O after the tax and banking details are verified.

## How long does approval take?

Most vendors are approved within three business days once documentation is complete.

## Who do I contact for invoice questions?

Email the Accounts Payable team or raise a ticket through the finance service desk.
